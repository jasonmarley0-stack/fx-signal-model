# Superseded — do not use for conclusions

This output was generated by the pre-correction version of the harness (commit `cc64fef`) and contained real, confirmed defects, corrected in the following commit:

1. **Timing bug**: signals were timestamped as published at their source H4 candle's OPEN, using data (close/high/low) only actually known at that candle's CLOSE (4 hours later) — entry-window checking could therefore start before the deciding candle had even completed.
2. **Scorer bug**: the entry candle itself was excluded from the post-entry stop/target scan (`index > entry_time` instead of `>=`), reintroducing a bug already fixed once in production `src/alert_scorer.py`.
3. **Holding-boundary mispricing**: a time exit was priced off a qualifying candle's CLOSE, which can represent an instant after the stated deadline; corrected to use that candle's OPEN.
4. **No gap handling**: a stop/target gapped through at a candle's own open was priced as an exact fill at the stated level, which was never actually quoted.
5. **Metric mislabeling**: `avg_net_r_per_entered_trade` was actually averaged over completed/resolved trades, not all entered trades; `avg_net_r_per_eligible_alert` silently excluded every unknown-outcome alert from its denominator despite its name.
6. **Baseline attribution bug**: the baseline candidate reimplemented `combine_signal()`'s formula by hand and omitted its spread floor (`MIN_RISK_SPREAD_MULTIPLE`) entirely, despite `RESULTS.md` describing it as importing `combine_signal()` directly.
7. **Position-occupancy bug**: `ambiguous_intrabar_exit` and `incomplete_coverage` outcomes (entered, but no confirmed exit) had no `exit_time_utc`, so the old occupancy check silently freed the pair for a new entry despite a real, unresolved position outstanding.

See `../RESULTS.md` for the corrected numbers and `../ISSUES.md` for the full list of what changed and why. The corrected run is deterministic and reproducible via `.venv/bin/python3 research/offline_comparison/run_experiment.py`.
