# Consolidated issue list — offline strategy comparison milestone

One list, covering the original milestone and both correction passes. Nothing here was allowed to expand scope beyond the three candidates already committed to.

## Fixed in the second correction pass (deadline-boundary rerun, after commit `819c41b`)

0. **A candle straddling the holding deadline could still resolve a stop/target/ambiguity via its full high/low.** Survived the first correction pass's entry-candle-inclusion fix (item 2 below) because it's a distinct bug: the completed-candle scan used `index <= scan_end` (a candle's OPEN at-or-before the deadline), not `index + interval <= scan_end` (the candle FULLY COMPLETED by the deadline) — so a candle whose span extended past the deadline could still have its high/low checked, letting up to ~30 minutes of *post-deadline* price action resolve a decision that should have been bounded by the deadline. Fixed: only fully-completed candles may contribute via high/low; the boundary candle (the one whose open lands *exactly* on the deadline — always exists when the data isn't gapped, since entries are always grid-aligned M30 opens and `max_holding_time_hours` is always a whole multiple of the M30 interval) may only contribute via its own open (gap-check, or the time-exit price); with no exact-deadline quote, the outcome is `incomplete_coverage`, never a fabricated fill from an earlier stale open. Five new tests in `test_deadline_boundary.py`. Effect: small, consistent with scope — full-window avg R/completed trade moved baseline +0.0866→+0.0859, genuine_orb +0.0072→+0.0083, trend_pullback −0.0620→−0.0613; no recommendation changed. Corrected the one pre-existing test whose assertion depended on the old (wrong) behaviour (`test_holding_boundary_prices_off_open_not_close`, renamed and reasserted to expect `incomplete_coverage` for a non-grid-aligned deadline).

**Not implemented in this pass:** a corresponding "prospective observation collector" (real-time practice bid/ask recording, a runbook, a paper scorer) was described alongside this fix in the review that prompted it, but was not built here — it's Stage 2 of the roadmap, a materially larger and separately-scoped deliverable (a long-running collector process, operational runbook, its own review cadence) that wasn't part of this specific correction order. Flagged for explicit request before starting.

## Fixed in the first correction pass (commit after `cc64fef`) — compromised the experiment if left

1. **Signals were timestamped at their source candle's open, not its completion.** `replay_engine.py` computed a decision from an H4 candle's close/high/low but published it at that candle's own opening timestamp — entry-window checking could begin before the deciding candle had even closed. Fixed: `decision_time = source_candle_start + 4h`, computed directly from the candle's own fixed duration (never inferred from the next available row in the dataset, which would be wrong across a data gap such as a weekend). Four separate fields now recorded per alert: `source_candle_start_utc`, `source_candle_completion_utc`, `decision_time_utc`, `earliest_permitted_entry_time_utc`. Effect: this alone eliminated most of what looked like an M30-resolution data-quality limit — `insufficient_data_entry` fell from ~30–40% of alerts to essentially zero.
2. **`replay_scorer.py` reintroduced the entry-candle-skip bug.** `index > entry_time` excluded the very candle that confirmed entry from the post-entry stop/target scan — the same bug already fixed once in production `src/alert_scorer.py`, reintroduced when this module was written from scratch for the research harness. Fixed to `>=`.
3. **Holding-time exits were priced off a candle's close, which can be after the deadline.** Candle timestamps denote opens; a qualifying candle's close represents an instant one full interval later, which can fall after the stated holding boundary. Fixed to price off that candle's open — the latest price actually known at or before the deadline.
4. **No gap handling for stop/target fills.** A level reached because the candle's own open had already gapped past it was priced as an exact fill at that level, which was never actually quoted. Fixed: gap-through fills price at the candle's own open; a normal intrabar touch (open not yet past the level) still fills at the exact level.
5. **Two headline metrics were mislabeled.** `avg_net_r_per_entered_trade` was actually averaged over completed/resolved trades (130), not all entered trades (160); `avg_net_r_per_eligible_alert` silently excluded every unknown-outcome alert from its denominator (142 known-outcome cases, not 244 eligible alerts) despite its name. Fixed: every averaged metric's key now states its exact denominator (`avg_net_r_per_completed_trade`, `avg_net_r_per_known_outcome_incl_missed_at_zero`), and the true all-eligible-alert average is reported as explicitly `avg_net_r_per_all_eligible_alert_is_undetermined=True` whenever any eligible alert's outcome is unknown, rather than silently substituting the known-outcome figure. `target_hit_rate_of_completed` and `profitable_trade_rate_of_completed` are now reported as two distinct metrics (the first pass only had a single ambiguous "win_rate").
6. **The baseline candidate reimplemented `combine_signal()` by hand and omitted the spread floor.** `RESULTS.md` claimed baseline "imports combine_signal() directly," but `replay_engine.py`'s `_baseline_or_orb_levels()` actually hand-rolled the confidence/SL-TP formula and never called the real function — silently omitting `MIN_RISK_SPREAD_MULTIPLE`'s spread floor entirely. Fixed: `_combiner_levels()` now calls the real, frozen `src/combiner.py::combine_signal()` (with `alpha=1.0`, `pestle_score=0.0`, matching `live_scanner.py`'s actual deployed config, not `combine_signal()`'s own default `alpha=0.6`) for both baseline and genuine_orb. Verified with a direct parity test against `combine_signal()`'s own output and a test confirming the spread floor actually binds on tight-ATR inputs.
7. **Ambiguous/incomplete-coverage entered positions silently freed the pair.** An entered position resolving to `ambiguous_intrabar_exit` or `incomplete_coverage` has no `exit_time_utc` at all — the old occupancy check only recognised `state=="open"` or a present `exit_time_utc`, matching neither, so the pair was freed for a new entry despite a real, unresolved position outstanding. Fixed: any entered position without a clean `exit_time_utc` conservatively reserves the pair through `entry_time + max_holding_time_hours`, flagged per suppressed row via `conservative_occupancy=True`. This reduced opportunity counts for every candidate (fewer bars are eligible for a new entry while a position is conservatively held) — a real, acknowledged trade-off, not a side effect to hide.

Re-ran the full pre-existing `alert-lifecycle-prospective-scoring` test suite (5 files) after all seven fixes — still passing, unmodified.

## Fixed in the original milestone (superseded numbers, fix still applies)

8. **`by_month()` silently omitted zero-opportunity months.** Fixed to always emit every month in range, zero-filled where nothing fired.
9. **`classify_and_record()` had no way to set a non-default entry-validity window**, so the harness's documented 4-hour contract silently fell back to the live scanner's 90-minute default. Added `entry_validity_minutes` (default unchanged, live scanner unaffected).

## Deliberate scope boundaries (not defects)

10. **Position-open suppression is total, not partial** — no cosmetic no-op, revision, or cancellation bookkeeping occurs for a pair while a position (confirmed or conservatively assumed) is open. A future iteration studying "what did the feed say while a subscriber was holding" would need to relax this.
11. **Sensitivity covers cost and a 30-minute delay only** — not `ENTRY_TOLERANCE_ATR_MULTIPLE`, not `MAX_HOLDING_TIME_HOURS`, not the confidence thresholds. Widening this is a parameter sweep, out of scope.
12. **Outcome/exit checking uses M30 candle resolution**, matching `performance_scorer.py`'s existing precedent, not tick data.

## Deferred for a later iteration (recorded, not actioned)

13. **`incomplete_coverage` (now the dominant unknown-outcome category, since the entry-timing fix eliminated most `insufficient_data_entry` cases) is concentrated around weekend gaps** — `MAX_HOLDING_TIME_HOURS=30` frequently lands the holding boundary inside the Friday-evening-to-Sunday-evening FX closure. Real, correctly-handled (no price fabricated), worth revisiting whether a holding-time exit should be defined in trading hours rather than wall-clock hours.
14. **The conservative-occupancy rule (item 7) trades opportunity count for correctness** — by design, and stated as such in `RESULTS.md`, but a later milestone could investigate a less conservative (evidence-based, not maximal) occupancy estimate once more prospective data exists to calibrate it against.
15. **Sample sizes remain modest in some holdout slices** even after correction (e.g. baseline holdout: 58 entries / 49 completed). Stated in `RESULTS.md`'s recommendation section, not treated as settled evidence either way.
16. **`replay_scorer.py` duplicates (and must be kept in sync with) `src/alert_scorer.py`'s state machine** rather than parameterising the production module to accept bid/ask series — a future fix to `alert_scorer.py`'s entry/exit logic would need to be mirrored here by hand.
